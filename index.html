Step 6: Faith as Authority

Conviction can impose meaning onto the supernatural.

Faith does not have to be religious. It may be religion, scientific certainty, moral duty, philosophy, ancestral belief, or personal conviction.

Roll: Power + Presence or Power + Persuasion

Banishment Through Faith

Roll: Power + Will or Power + Resolve

Faith-based actions generate Corruption and may generate Attention from forces aligned with or opposed to the belief being invoked.
